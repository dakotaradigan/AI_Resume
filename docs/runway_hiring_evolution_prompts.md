# Runway Prompts — "The Evolution of Hiring" (Flip Book / Super Bowl Spot)

A cinematic flip-book spot that parodies the classic "March of Progress" (evolution of
man) image: each era of job hunting is a hominid stage, ending with the fully upright
human whose AI agent does the applying.

Runway generates short clips (5–10s), so the spot is built as one clip per era plus a
final tableau, stitched in an editor. Paste the **Master Style Block** into every
generation so the look stays consistent, then add the scene prompt.

---

## Master Style Block (prepend to every scene prompt)

> Cinematic Super Bowl commercial, shot on anamorphic 35mm, 2.39:1 letterbox, shallow
> depth of field, dramatic key lighting, rich film grain. Stop-motion flip-book
> aesthetic: the scene lives on thick paper pages that flip rapidly, edges visible,
> slight hand-drawn jitter between frames. Warm nostalgic color grade that cools and
> sharpens as eras advance. No text, no logos.

---

## Scene 1 — 1978: The Handshake (the crouched ancestor)

> A man in a corduroy suit with a mustache hunches forward as he hands a typewritten
> paper resume across a wood-paneled desk to a hiring manager, firm handshake, cigarette
> smoke in the air, amber tungsten light, filing cabinets behind them. His posture is
> stooped like an early hominid. Page-flip transition at the end of the shot.

## Scene 2 — 1997: The Attachment (rising slightly)

> A slightly more upright office worker squints at a beige CRT monitor in a fluorescent
> cubicle, attaching "resume.doc" to an email, dial-up modem lights blinking, the same
> resume now glowing on the screen. Cooler fluorescent-green grade, CRT flicker. He
> stands a little straighter than before. Rapid flip-book page transition.

## Scene 3 — 2005: The PDF (walking upright)

> A business-casual worker at a silver laptop in a glass-walled office exports the same
> resume to PDF, a crisp document icon shimmering like a fossil preserved in amber.
> Cleaner daylight grade, confident posture, almost fully upright. Subtle flip-book
> paper jitter, page-flip transition.

## Scene 4 — 2014: The Form (evolutionary setback)

> A modern open-plan office. An exhausted applicant uploads their PDF to a job portal —
> then the camera pushes in as endless web form fields cascade down the screen, forcing
> them to re-type the same resume box by box. Their upright posture collapses back into
> a hunch, forehead to desk. Cold blue screen-glow grade, comedic despair, Super Bowl
> commercial timing. Frantic page-flip transition.

## Scene 5 — 2026: The Agent (fully evolved)

> A relaxed person stands fully upright by a sunlit window drinking coffee while a soft
> glowing orb of light — an AI agent — converses with floating interview panels and
> assembles their resume into place behind them like constellation points. They never
> touch a keyboard. Golden-hour hero lighting, slow push-in, triumphant. The flip-book
> pages settle and go still.

## Scene 6 — Final Tableau: The March of Progress

> Classic "evolution of man" silhouette lineup in left-to-right profile against a clean
> parchment background, rendered as a flip-book illustration: first figure crouched
> holding a paper resume, second hunched over a CRT monitor, third upright with a
> laptop, fourth collapsed backward over a web form, final figure standing tall and
> empty-handed while a small glowing agent orb hovers beside it doing the work. Camera
> slowly dollies right along the lineup, pages fluttering at the edges. Room left at the
> end for a tagline card.

## Single-Generation Version (if you only want one clip)

> Cinematic Super Bowl commercial in a stop-motion flip-book style, anamorphic 35mm,
> 2.39:1. A "March of Progress" evolution-of-man lineup where each figure is a job
> seeker: a crouched 1970s man handing over a paper resume, a hunched 1990s worker at a
> beige CRT emailing a Word doc, an upright 2000s worker with a PDF on a laptop, a
> figure slumped over an endless web application form, and finally a fully upright
> person standing free while a glowing AI agent orb beside them assembles the resume in
> midair. Camera dollies left to right along the lineup as flip-book pages turn rapidly,
> lighting evolving from warm tungsten to clean golden-hour. Dramatic, funny, triumphant.
> No text.

## Production Tips

- **Consistency:** Runway keeps character/style consistency best with image-to-video.
  Generate one still of the Scene 6 tableau first (Runway's image tool or Midjourney),
  then feed crops of each figure as the keyframe for its scene.
- **Prompt length:** Runway responds best to a single dense paragraph; trim the scene
  prompts rather than adding more clauses if results drift.
- **The flip:** If the flip-book effect fights the realism, drop it from the scene
  prompts and add page-flip transitions in the edit instead — keep it only in Scene 6.
- **Sound design (in edit):** typewriter clacks → dial-up screech → mouse clicks →
  frantic keyboard → silence and one soft chime for the agent. The dial-up tone is the
  laugh line.
- **Tagline slot:** Scene 6 ends with dead air on parchment — drop the product name and
  tagline there, e.g. "Stop applying. Start evolving."
