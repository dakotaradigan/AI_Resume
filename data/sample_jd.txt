Senior Product Manager, AI Platform

About the role
You will own the roadmap for the AI platform that our investment and client teams build on: retrieval, evaluation and agent tooling. You will work with engineering, compliance and client-facing teams every week.

Requirements
- 7+ years of product management experience, including at least three years shipping machine learning or large language model products to production users
- Hands-on experience with retrieval-augmented generation, vector databases and evaluation frameworks for LLM applications
- Fluency in SQL and Python
- Experience in asset management, wealth management or another regulated financial-services business
- Working knowledge of model risk management and AI governance
- Has managed a team of five or more product managers
- Strong cross-functional leadership and executive communication skills

Nice to have
- Experience building with the Model Context Protocol or agent frameworks
- An MBA or CFA charter
- Experience launching products to institutional clients

Benefits
Competitive salary, 401(k) match and hybrid work.
