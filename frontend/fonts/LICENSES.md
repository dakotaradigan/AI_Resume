# Self-hosted fonts

Both faces are licensed under the SIL Open Font License 1.1 and redistributed
here with their upstream license texts. They are served from this origin so
the site makes no third-party font requests.

| Family | Role | Copyright | License |
|---|---|---|---|
| Manrope | Display face (`--font-display`) | © 2018 The Manrope Project Authors | [OFL-manrope.txt](OFL-manrope.txt) |
| Inter | Body and UI face (`--font-body`) | © 2020 The Inter Project Authors | [OFL-inter.txt](OFL-inter.txt) |

Files are latin and latin-ext WOFF2 subsets of the upstream releases with
variable weight axes. `fonts.css` carries the `@font-face` declarations.
